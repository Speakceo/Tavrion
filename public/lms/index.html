What is a learning management system?

A learning management system (LMS) is software used to create, deliver, track, and report on employee training. Tavrion LMS goes beyond traditional LMS features with AI mock calls, live coaching, hiring assessments (Tavrion Test), multi-organisation tenancy, and real-time analytics — so you can train and measure capability, not just assign courses.
